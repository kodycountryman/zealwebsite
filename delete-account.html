Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your Zeal+ account and data. Questions? support@zealplus.app

You can permanently delete your Zeal+ account and all associated data directly from within the app — no need to contact support.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Zeal+ app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & All Data.
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete Everything.

This action is immediate and cannot be undone. Once confirmed, your account and data are permanently erased and you're signed out.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Workout history, logs, sets, reps, and personal records
  • Training plans, streaks, and progress data
  • Profile information — fitness level, equipment, body metrics
  • Run tracking history and GPS routes
  • Any connected Health/Health Connect sync data stored in our systems

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited data for a short period where required by law or for legitimate business purposes, such as:

  • Transaction records related to subscription purchases (retained per financial record-keeping requirements)
  • Aggregated, de-identified data that can no longer be linked to you

All other personal data is deleted or fully anonymized within 30 days of your deletion request, consistent with our Privacy Policy.

Can't Access the App?

If you're unable to sign in or use the in-app deletion flow, email support@zealplus.app from the address associated with your account and we'll process the deletion manually within 30 days.
